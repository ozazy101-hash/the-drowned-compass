# 03 — Softly claim a Character Slot

**What to build:** A player can select one of the six unclaimed Character Slots, complete the short two-step setup, and arrive on a newly initialized Character Page without creating a hard ownership restriction.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Enter the protected Party.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Step one captures player name, character name, primary class, subclass, species, background, and level.
- [ ] Step two captures all six Ability Scores and prevents incomplete or invalid scores from being submitted.
- [ ] Completing setup marks the Character Slot as claimed and opens its Character Page.
- [ ] The claim identifies the responsible player while every authenticated Party member retains editing access.
- [ ] Another signed-in browser receives the claimed identity without reloading.
