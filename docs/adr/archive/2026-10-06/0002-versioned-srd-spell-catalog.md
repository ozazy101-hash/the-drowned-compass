# Use a versioned SRD 5.2.1 spell catalog

The prototype will bundle a validated, versioned catalog containing only spells released in SRD 5.2.1 under CC BY 4.0, with the required attribution, rather than scrape D&D Beyond or depend on a third-party API during play. Players may add missing spells as character-specific Custom Spells, which preserves flexibility without reproducing non-SRD descriptions whose reuse has not been licensed.
