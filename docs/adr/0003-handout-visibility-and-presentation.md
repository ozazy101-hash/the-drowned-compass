# Separate Handout visibility from presentation

Handouts belong to one collection with private or revealed visibility, rather than separate private and public databases. The Dungeon Master can prepare content privately, reveal it for persistent Party Library access, and independently choose the Handout shown on the Party Display; presenting private content also reveals it. This avoids duplicated files and couples neither the players' independent reading nor persistent access to what happens to be on the TV.

Only the Dungeon Master manages visibility and presentation. Access must be enforced by backend and storage permissions. Withdrawal removes Party access and clears an active presentation, but cannot revoke material players have already copied.

Replacing a Handout preserves its visibility and updates revealed content directly. The action is labelled Replace for everyone for revealed material; a draft-and-publish version workflow was rejected to keep the DM workflow light.
